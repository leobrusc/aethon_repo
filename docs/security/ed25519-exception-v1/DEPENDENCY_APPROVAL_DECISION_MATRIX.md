# Decision model — proposals only

| Proposed outcome | Objective criteria | Required independent human evidence | Current applicability |
|---|---|---|---|
| ACCEPTABLE_WITH_COMPENSATING_CONTROLS | Exact hash/platform/use scope; no integrity/invalid-signature issue; relevant components bounded; notice applicability resolved; pre-install compensating controls evidenced/tested; post-install checks explicitly bound to later operational approval; residual risk justified; explicit expiry/rollback | Named empowered risk owner and distinct independent reviewer, authentic approval reference bound to dossier/policy hash, UTC validity; competent compliance disposition | Not proposed acceptable for any current record; prerequisites incomplete |
| ADDITIONAL_EVIDENCE_REQUIRED | Defined material gap with a specific evidence request; no evidence of integrity rejection, but acceptance criteria not met | Same accountable identification and independent decision evidence before this becomes an effective human disposition | Proposed for all five exception records; actual decisions PENDING |
| REJECTED | Digest/identity mismatch, invalid/adulterated attestation, disallowed component/use, unacceptable legal risk or failed mandatory control; an empowered owner explicitly rejects | Identified owner, reason and independent authenticated record; software may separately stop on a technical failure without inventing this human decision | No such material artifact divergence demonstrated; no human rejection recorded |
| NOT_ASSESSED | No adequate assessment completed or scope not yet defined | Identified owner/reviewer must document deferral and limitations; never authorizes use | Future exact runtime and unexamined OS resolution remain unassessed |

These are proposal criteria, not an automated approval engine or existing project policy amendment. Effective outcomes remain PENDING until real responsible persons are identified and independent approval evidence is validated. The person performing collection/preparation cannot self-approve as a second reviewer under an alias; authority, identity and independence must be evidenced. No tool output, test PASS, matching hash, signed publisher attestation or filled boolean constitutes risk acceptance.

Each signed/reference-backed decision must bind record ID, exact filename/version/hash/platform/native member where relevant, intended use, policy version/hash, evidence index hash, named authority and independent reviewer, rationale, residual risks, required implemented controls and UTC valid_from/valid_until. No inherited approval across Windows/Linux or across provenance/native/license issues. Effective installation additionally requires explicit dependency/runtime authority; exception acceptance alone is insufficient.

Follow the two-stage runtime plan: a human may later explicitly accept a narrowly scoped exception for separately authorized controlled installation/evidence collection only, with all applicable pre-install controls satisfied. Installed-SBOM and authorized runtime-test results can then be produced and independently assessed before operational approval. Do not require post-install evidence before that limited installation permission, and do not treat its absence as permission to operate. Neither stage is approved here.

Any hash/version/platform/runtime/policy change, material advisory/compromise, expired date or missing/revoked approval requires new evaluation. Expiry has no grace or automatic renewal; suspend future installation/use authorization on trigger and let accountable incident/risk owners assess any existing deployment. No existing deployment is created here.

Current recommendation **EVIDENCE_GAPS_REMAIN**: complete the material native/notice evidence or have the empowered owner determine whether narrowly scoped additional exception consideration is permissible. Do not convert uncertainty into low probability or blanket acceptance. Three provenance forms are independent; two native forms and legal closure are separate blockers to a later installation decision.

Effective decisions, named owners, independent reviewers, signatures and approval evidence remain **PENDING**. **DEPENDENCY_APPROVAL_REQUIRED**; **PILOT_BLOCKED**, all eight operational gates PENDING. This dossier does not authorize installation, locks, runtime provisioning, adapter implementation or pilot execution.
